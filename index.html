Who we are

A legacy is more than what we leave behind. It is what we build together.

Next Generation Legacy (NGL) and Kingdom Fields are connected expressions of one larger vision: develop people while building the practical systems communities need to thrive.

We believe lasting social impact takes more than inspiration. It takes character, knowledge, productive work, local enterprise, responsible leadership, and collaboration that turns ideas into action.

The long-term vision

Kingdom Fields:
a place where a vision becomes a living system.

Kingdom Fields is envisioned as a connected, community-oriented ecosystem where human development and productive enterprise reinforce one another. The long-term concept may include agricultural production, learning and mentorship spaces, enterprise development, health and well-being, logistics, storage, distribution, and community gathering places.

This is a future-facing concept. Specific facilities, projects, timelines, funding, and operating status should be added only when confirmed.
